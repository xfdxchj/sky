# P3.4 Test Plan — Mob Catalogue and Incoming Damage

## Data loading
1. Run `gradlew build` with Java 21.
2. Launch Minecraft 1.21.1 Fabric.
3. Confirm log reports 51 mob definitions and 5 unique vanilla entity mappings (Zombie, Skeleton, Spider, Zombie Villager, Wolf).
4. Run `/skyblock mob list`.
5. Inspect `private_island_zombie`, `private_island_skeleton`, `private_island_spider`, `graveyard_zombie`, `zombie_villager`, `crypt_ghoul`, `golden_ghoul`, `wolf`, and `old_wolf` with `/skyblock mob info <id>`.
6. Inspect representative variants with `/skyblock mob info zombie_15`, `/skyblock mob info skeleton_14` and `/skyblock mob info spider_15`; confirm their individual levels and stats differ from the first variant.
7. Confirm drops with unknown chance display as unknown rather than 0%.

## Incoming damage
1. Spawn a vanilla Zombie. Allow it to hit the player without armor. The configured raw mob Damage is 20 before vanilla mitigation.
2. Repeat with Skeleton (15) and Spider (35).
3. Spawn a Zombie Villager (24) and Wolf (75); verify their dedicated mappings.
4. Equip armor or apply Resistance and confirm vanilla mitigation still applies after the configured raw damage value is supplied.
5. Confirm fall, fire, drowning, cactus, and other environmental damage are unchanged.
6. Confirm player-vs-player damage is unchanged.
7. Confirm an unregistered hostile mob uses vanilla damage.

## Regression
1. Confirm player SkyBlock weapon damage and custom mob HP still work.
2. Confirm P3.2 commands and item registry still work.
3. Confirm rewards remain as configured for the three mapped starter mobs.

## Known limitations
- This milestone does not assign different level variants to naturally spawned entities. The catalogue contains variant records where available, but variant-aware spawn selection is a later milestone.
- Catalogue-only mobs do not yet receive custom incoming damage.
- SkyBlock player Defense is not yet applied to mob hits.
- Mob drop tables are descriptive data only; the vanilla loot system is unchanged.
- Full local build and in-game validation are required before marking P3.4 verified.
