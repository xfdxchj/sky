# P3.3 Test Plan — SkyBlock Mob Health

## Build and startup
1. Run `gradlew build` using Java 21.
2. Launch Minecraft 1.21.1 with Fabric and create/open a test world.
3. Confirm P3.2 item and mob registries still load (3 mob definitions, 8 item definitions).

## Registered mob HP
1. Equip `training_sword`.
2. Spawn a vanilla Zombie (the current starter analogue) and attack once.
3. Confirm the action bar reports SkyBlock HP as `current/maximum`, starting from 100 maximum, rather than the vanilla 20 HP scale.
4. With the current test weapon (expected 16 non-critical damage against Defense 0), confirm the HP pool decreases by approximately 16, subject to Minecraft damage mitigation and hit cooldown.
5. Repeat until death. Confirm the mob does not die merely because its vanilla health would otherwise have reached zero early; it dies when authoritative SkyBlock HP reaches zero.
6. Confirm ordinary vanilla drops/death behavior still occurs and configured rewards are granted once.

## Persistence
1. Damage a registered mob without killing it.
2. Save and quit the world, then reopen it.
3. Locate the same surviving mob and attack again.
4. Confirm its remaining SkyBlock HP is restored from world PersistentState and does not reset to full.

## Regression
1. Attack an unregistered living entity with the SkyBlock weapon; confirm it retains vanilla health behavior and receives no configured reward.
2. Attack with no SkyBlock weapon; confirm vanilla combat remains unchanged.
3. Confirm PvP is still not intercepted.
4. Recheck `/skyblock mob list`, `/skyblock mob info private_island_zombie`, `/skyblock item info training_sword`, `/skyblock combat`.

## Known limitations
- SkyBlock HP is persistent by entity UUID in dimension-scoped PersistentState. Dead entities killed through this custom combat path have their entry removed; entities removed by unrelated vanilla mechanics can leave stale entries.
- Vanilla health is synchronized as a proportional visual representation. Vanilla damage mitigation and invulnerability frames still influence accepted damage.
- Mob attack damage/AI, custom nameplates, boss bars, spawn tables and custom entity types are not implemented.
- Mob HP values in current starter JSON remain subject to data-source verification.
