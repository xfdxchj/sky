# P3.4 Design — Mob Catalogue and Incoming Damage

## Goals
- Expand mob JSON records beyond combat numbers: family, category, mob types, area, drops and provenance.
- Add a sourced starter catalogue covering Private Island, Graveyard, Crypts and Ruins examples.
- Apply configured SkyBlock mob Damage when a mapped mob damages a player.
- Preserve vanilla player-side damage mitigation, armor, resistance and invulnerability handling.

## Mob data model
Each record stores:
- Stable internal ID and family ID
- Optional vanilla entity type bridge
- Level, Health, Damage and Defense
- Coin and Combat XP rewards
- Category, one or more Mob Types and spawn area
- Drop entries with min/max count and probability
- Source type, source URL, retrieval date and confidence note

A drop probability is only considered known when `chance_known=true`. Unknown probabilities must not be interpreted as a zero-percent drop or as a guaranteed drop.

## Incoming damage
A LivingEntity damage-argument modifier checks whether the target is a server player. It resolves the source's attacker (including projectile owner fallback) through the entity-type bridge. If a registered mob has a positive configured Damage value, that value replaces the vanilla raw damage input. Minecraft then continues its normal damage pipeline.

This is an initial compatibility bridge. It does not yet implement custom mob AI, attack cadence, hitboxes, projectile behavior, SkyBlock player Defense, or per-instance variant selection. Mob entries without an entity_type are catalogue-only.

## Known scope
- The catalogue includes all 15 listed Private Island variants for Zombie, Skeleton and Spider, plus sourced examples from the Graveyard, Crypts, Ruins, The Park and Deep Caverns. The first variant in each family maps to its vanilla entity type; other level variants remain catalogue-only until variant-aware spawn assignment is implemented.
- Graveyard Zombie, Crypt Ghoul, Golden Ghoul and Old Wolf are catalogue-only because they share vanilla entity types with other mobs and must not be mapped ambiguously.
- Zombie Villager and Wolf have dedicated vanilla entity type mappings.
- Mob Defense remains unverified where the wiki does not list it; zero is a project placeholder in the current combat formula.
